About

// context

I'm a Software Engineering student at Arizona State University (expected May 2028), currently interning at Diamond Kitchen and Bath in Phoenix, where I split time between IT infrastructure work and building internal tools.

Most of what I build sits at the seam between two systems that were never designed to talk to each other — an identity provider and an asset tracker, a phone call and a CRM, an audit log and a dashboard. I like that problem: it's less about writing new logic and more about making a handoff reliable.

Outside of work, I'm working through Princeton's Algorithms (Sedgewick) course and ASU's data structures coursework, and I'm currently focused on landing a Summer 2027 internship in AI/ML or backend engineering.
